# Stakeholder Analysis

| Stakeholder | Role | Interest/Expectations | Influence on the System | How the System Affects the Stakeholder |
|-------------|------|------------------------|--------------------------|------------------------------------------|
| Airport Operations Staff | End user; monitors overall airport activity and flight status via the dashboard | Wants an accurate, up-to-date, unified view of all flights, bays, and runways; expects the system to be reliable and easy to interpret at a glance | Their daily workflow and feedback directly shape UI/dashboard design and what data is prioritized on screen | Reduces manual tracking effort and coordination overhead, but a bug or unclear status display could lead to poor operational decisions |
| Ground Control Personnel | End user; manages runway assignment and aircraft movement coordination | Needs real-time, accurate runway availability and fast updates when assignments change; expects minimal delay between action and reflected status | Their operational rules directly influence the runway management logic the team must build | Speeds up runway coordination and reduces double-booking risk, but if the system lags or shows wrong data, it could cause real coordination errors |
| Development Team | Builds, tests, and maintains the system as a course project | Wants to deliver a stable, functional system that genuinely solves the coordination problem; expects clear requirements from stakeholders and reasonable timelines to build a quality product | Directly controls feature scope, architecture, and implementation quality; team decisions determine what gets built and how | They're responsible for ongoing maintenance, bug fixes, and future feature requests once the system is in use |
