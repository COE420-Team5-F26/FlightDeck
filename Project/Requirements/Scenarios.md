# Scenario Identification

| Scenario ID | Scenario Title | Actor/Stakeholder | Scenario Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| S-01 | Reassigning Runway After a Weather Delay | Ground Control Personnel | A storm forces the closure of Runway A. Ground control opens FlightDeck, sees that Runway A is marked "closed," and reassigns the three flights scheduled to depart from it to Runway B. The system checks that Runway B has no conflicting time windows before confirming each new assignment, and the updated runway occupancy is immediately visible to airport operations staff on the dashboard. |
| S-02 | Airline Coordinator Checking a Flight's Bay and Status | Airline Coordinator | An airline coordinator for Emirates needs to know where flight EK-123 will park and whether it is on time. She logs into FlightDeck, filters the flight list to her airline, and finds EK-123 marked "delayed" with an assigned bay number and a timestamp showing when the status was last updated, letting her relay accurate information to ground staff. |
| S-03 | Two Ground Controllers Assigning the Same Runway | Ground Control Personnel | During a busy departure window, two ground controllers at different stations both attempt to assign Runway A to two different flights within the same 10 minute slot. When the second controller submits the assignment, FlightDeck detects the overlap and displays a conflict alert instead of silently overwriting the first assignment, so the controllers can coordinate manually and pick a different runway or time. |
| S-04 | Correcting an Outdated Flight Status | Airport Operations Staff | A flight has technically landed, but the automatic status sync from the tower feed has not updated FlightDeck yet, still showing "in air" ten minutes after touchdown. An operations staff member confirms the landing visually, manually overrides the flight's status to "landed," and the system timestamps the override with her user ID so that anyone reviewing the record later knows the correction was made manually. |
| S-05 | Monitoring Overall Airport Activity During Peak Hours | Airport Operations Staff | During the evening peak, an operations staff member needs a single view of everything happening at once. She opens the centralized dashboard, which shows all active flights with their statuses, all runway occupancy, and all bay allocations in one screen, and filters it to show only flights currently in the "boarding" or "delayed" state to prioritize which situations need attention first. |
